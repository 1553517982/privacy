1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Cosmic Shredder (the “Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), a mobile sci-fi action and base defense game published on the Google Play Store.

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect when you install, play, or interact with our game, how that data is used, safeguarded, and shared, and the choices and rights available to you under applicable data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement & Policies.

Key Guarantee: We do NOT sell your personal data. We do not collect real-name personal identities, physical addresses, or phone numbers. Core gameplay is completely playable in offline mode, and all non-essential analytics and diagnostic data collections are subject to your granular in-game control.

3. Information We Collect

We follow the principle of data minimization. The data we process falls into the following categories:

A. Technical & Device Information (Automatically Processed)

When you launch and run the Game, certain technical identifiers and environment attributes are automatically processed by your device and underlying platform services:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Play Advertising ID (GAID), Firebase App Instance ID, or pseudonymous installation tokens.
  • Device Hardware & OS: Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, CPU architecture, screen resolution, GPU model, and system language/locale.
  • Network & Location: Coarse geographic region inferred from truncated IP address (country or city level, for regional ad compliance and content CDN routing). We never collect precise GPS location.

B. Gameplay & Progression Data

To ensure a persistent and seamless gameplay experience, the Game saves your progress:

  • Local Save Files: Your campaign sector clearances, camp defensive structures, workshop refits, vehicle modules, and in-game currencies are primarily stored locally on your device.
  • Optional Cloud Sync: If you connect via Google Play Games Services, high-level save checkpoints and achievements are synchronized to your Google account.

C. In-App Purchases & Financial Information

In-game purchases (such as Supporter Packs or cosmetics) are conducted strictly through Google Play In-App Billing.

No Financial Data Collection: We do NOT collect, receive, or store credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses. All billing transactions are securely handled by Google Play. We only receive pseudonymous purchase tokens, order IDs, product IDs, and purchase timestamps to validate and unlock your digital entitlements.

D. Analytics & Telemetry Data (User-Controlled / Opt-in)

If enabled in your privacy settings, we collect pseudonymous gameplay metrics via Google Firebase Analytics to balance combat difficulty and improve game design (e.g., sector attempts, wave completions, crash points). You may disable analytics at any time.

E. Diagnostics & Crash Reports (User-Controlled / Opt-in)

If enabled, anonymized stack traces, crash dumps, and device state reports are generated via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs and performance bottlenecks.

5. How We Use Your Information & Legal Bases

Under the GDPR and international data protection laws, we process personal information only when we have a valid legal basis:

  • Performance of a Contract: Processing purchase verification tokens and maintaining cloud save sync to deliver the game services and digital items you acquire.
  • Consent: Serving personalized ads via Google AdMob (where required by EEA/UK regulations), and gathering optional telemetry/analytics and diagnostics. You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests: Securing the application against fraud, abuse, or unauthorized exploitation, and delivering non-personalized contextual ads to support free-to-play operations.
  • Legal Obligations: Retaining mandatory accounting/transaction records in connection with in-app purchases.

7. In-Game Privacy Controls & Consent

We believe that privacy controls should be straightforward, transparent, and always accessible directly from within the game:

  • First Launch Privacy Choice: On the first game startup, you are presented with explicit options to enable or decline Analytics and Diagnostic telemetry before any optional metrics are sent.
  • In-Game Settings Menu: At any point during gameplay, you can navigate to Settings → Data Privacy to toggle Analytics and Diagnostics on or off. Changes take effect immediately.
  • Advertising Consent Management: In regions governed by GDPR/UK regulations, an “Ad Privacy Options” button powered by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) allows you to review and revise your ad tracking choices at will.
  • Operating System Ad Tracking: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to accomplish the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law (such as tax or financial audit regulations).

How to Delete Your Data

In accordance with the Google Play Account Deletion Policy, you have full control over your stored credentials and data:

  • Self-Service Web Deletion: You can visit our official public deletion portal at any time to permanently delete your linked cloud account and associated database records:
    Open Account Deletion Portal →
  • Local Game Data: You can delete all local save data immediately by clearing the app’s cache and storage in Android Settings → Apps → Cosmic Shredder → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Email Request: You may also request complete data deletion by emailing privacy@cosmicshredder.com with your pseudonymous in-game ID or Google Play account details. Requests are verified and completed within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

Our Game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 for residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom).

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under these age limits. If we discover that personal data from a child has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete such data from our records. If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@cosmicshredder.com.

10. Security of Your Information

We implement rigorous technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration:

  • All communications with backend services and Google APIs utilize industry-standard TLS 1.3/HTTPS encryption in transit.
  • Cloud functions and databases enforce least-privilege role-based access controls and Firestore security rules.
  • No raw payment or financial data ever touches our servers.

While no transmission over the internet is completely infallible, we continuously assess and upgrade our security practices to protect your data.

11. Your Rights (GDPR, UK DPA, & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, you enjoy specific legal rights regarding your personal information:

European Economic Area (EEA) & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR):

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing & Portability: Request limitations on processing or data transfer to another service.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously granted consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA):

  • Right to Know & Access: Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information collected from you.
  • Do Not Sell or Share: We do NOT sell or share your personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: You will never receive discriminatory treatment or diminished game services for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@cosmicshredder.com.
